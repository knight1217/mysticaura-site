Tarot for Beginners: Understanding the Major Arcana

Published June 2026 — MysticAura Editorial

What Is Tarot?

A traditional tarot deck contains 78 cards divided into the Major Arcana (22 cards) and Minor Arcana (56 cards). The Major Arcana represents life's big themes and spiritual lessons — the Fool's journey from innocence to enlightenment.

The Fool's Journey

The 22 Major Arcana cards tell a story. The Fool (0) begins as an innocent traveler, encountering the Magician, High Priestess, and eventually confronting Death, the Tower, and the World. Each card marks a stage of personal growth and transformation.

Key Major Arcana Cards

The Fool (0)

New beginnings, spontaneity, faith in the universe. A leap into the unknown with an open heart.

The Lovers (VI)

Love, harmony, choices. Not just romantic love — deep alignment of values.

Death (XIII)

Transformation, endings, change. Rarely physical death — usually a profound personal metamorphosis.

The Star (XVII)

Hope, renewal, serenity. The calm after the storm. A beautiful card of healing.

The World (XXI)

Completion, accomplishment, wholeness. The end of one cycle and the beginning of another.

Your First Tarot Reading

Start with a simple three-card spread: Past, Present, Future. Shuffle while focusing on your question. Draw three cards left to right. The first card reveals influences from the past. The second shows your current situation. The third suggests what may come.
