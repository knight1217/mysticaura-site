Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Information We Collect

MysticAura uses Google Analytics (via cookies) to understand how visitors use our site. This includes pages visited, time on site, and general location (country/city level only). We do not collect personally identifiable information.

Our AI-powered tools receive the inputs you provide (zodiac sign, mood, etc.) and send them to our secure API for processing. These inputs are not stored or shared with third parties.

Cookies

We use cookies for Google Analytics and essential site functionality. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — Anonymous site usage statistics
  • Google AdSense — Display advertising (uses cookies for personalized ads)

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

AI Tool Data Handling

Our interactive tools (horoscope, compatibility, tarot, persona, destiny keywords, and inner element discovery) receive your inputs via HTTPS and process them through our secure API. Each request is processed in real time and the results are returned directly to your browser. We do not log, store, train AI models on, or share your inputs with any third party. Once your reading is generated, the input data is discarded.

Children's Privacy

MysticAura is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
