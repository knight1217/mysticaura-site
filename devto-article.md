## Why I built this

Every astrology app I found was the same: download, create account, give email, pay subscription, get daily push notifications. I just wanted to check my horoscope without becoming someone's marketing lead.

So I built a clean, private alternative. No accounts. No data collection. No notifications. Just you and the stars.

---

## What people actually use

After watching the analytics for a few weeks, the most popular tools are:

1. **Tarot Reading** — hands down #1. People love the card reveal animation.
2. **Zodiac Compatibility** — "Is my crush compatible?" is apparently a universal question.
3. **Daily Horoscope** — steady daily users who come back every morning.

---

## What I'd do differently

The site is intentionally minimal — and that's both a strength and a weakness. Some users want more detailed explanations, historical data, or the ability to save readings. I chose simplicity over features because I wanted something that works immediately without friction.

If you're building something: ship the simplest version first. You can always add features later. You can't add users later.
